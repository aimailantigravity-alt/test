BLOOD MATE



BLOOD MATE is a blood donation portal for blood donors and blood receivers. With BLOOD MATE, donors can easily find nearby blood banks,register to donate, and track their donations, Recipients can also find blood donors and receive blood quickly and safely. BLOOD MATE is helping the gap between those in need and those who can provide. Our BLOOD MATE Application currently works in COIMBATORE.
